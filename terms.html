Terms of use

TRAISTAR BROKER is a local business tool operated by TRAISTAR BROKER SRL for customs document preparation. Contact: traistarbroker@gmail.com.

Authorized business use

Use the application only with documents, mailboxes and registries that you are authorized to process. Google access requires the account holder's OAuth consent. The application does not grant access to another person's account merely because this website is public.

Review before operational use

OCR and AI extraction may contain errors. Product classifications and historical analogues require review for the actual goods. Users must resolve material discrepancies and check the current documents before generating or submitting a declaration. An XML file or a successful application check does not constitute acceptance, registration or release by ASYCUDAWorld or a customs authority.

Sending, synchronization and source files

OAuth permissions and operational sending are separate controls. Test mode blocks sending and Google writes. Operational mode and enabled network writes allow the authorized workflow to send final checked PDFs and update the existing registry. Source packages should be preserved, and checks performed on working copies. Maintain suitable backups.

Third-party services and privacy

Google and optional OpenAI services require their own access and are subject to their own availability and terms. Data practices are described in our Privacy policy. Google access can be revoked at any time from the connected account.
