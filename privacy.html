Privacy policy

This policy describes how the TRAISTAR BROKER local Windows application uses information. The application is operated by TRAISTAR BROKER SRL. Contact: traistarbroker@gmail.com.

Information accessed

The application processes documents deliberately selected for customs work, dossier identifiers, invoice and shipment references, amounts, weights and source references. With Google OAuth authorization it can read Gmail messages, headers and attachments to identify the source document thread. It can read the configured Google Sheets registry and, when operational writes are enabled, update dossier and declaration records.

Purpose and permitted actions

Gmail data is used to match source packages, establish the correct reply thread and participants, verify attachment checksums and detect prior successful sending. Gmail send permission is used for authorized delivery of final checked PDFs. Google Sheets data is used to synchronize the existing work registry. Operational sending and writes are controlled separately from OAuth permission and can remain disabled.

Local storage and security

Working copies, extracted text, reports, indexes, audit records and backups are stored on the operator's computer in configured folders. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored outside source code and encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Local documents and the local database are not all encrypted by this application; device access controls, disk encryption and backups remain the operator's responsibility. This public website does not host customer dossiers or OAuth credentials.

Sharing and optional AI

Google requests go to Google APIs. Authorized outgoing messages share the final PDF with the participants selected from the verified source thread. Optional AI extraction sends only explicitly selected document text to the OpenAI API after consent. Google mailbox contents are not automatically sent to an AI service. The application requests no model training and does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google data is not sold, used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

TRAISTAR BROKER's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local files, extracted information and backups remain until the operator removes them under the applicable business retention requirements. Disconnecting OAuth does not automatically remove previously saved local copies. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. To remove local credentials and data, stop the application and remove its relevant encrypted credential files and local data, including any backups that must also be deleted. Contact us for assistance with access, correction or deletion requests.

Website and third parties

This informational site has no account form, advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies. The hosting provider may process technical connection information needed to deliver website pages. Google and OpenAI process requests under their respective applicable policies and terms. This policy does not replace their policies.

Changes and contact

Material changes to application data practices will be reflected here and in the application. Questions: traistarbroker@gmail.com.
